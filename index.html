Download the AI models?

Chord-er can use two AI models for a better analysis:

  • Beat tracking (82 MB): follows tempo changes and finds the bars.
  • Stem separation (166 MB): isolates the harmony and the bass, for more accurate chords and slash chords.

They are downloaded once from Hugging Face and then kept in your browser's cache. Your audio never leaves your computer either way.

Without them you get the basic analysis: a steady-tempo beat grid and chords from the full mix.
